# Learn

## What

The goal is a better skill, not a record of what went wrong. A skill is the instructions you work from. When they fail you, fix the instructions. You are the worst judge of your own passes.

## When

- mike or a peer corrects you, or you make the same mistake twice
- Your own sense that a pass went badly is not enough
- No correction, no edit, and never a cadence retro

## How

- Fix the rule that failed, in place. Rewrite it, sharpen it, or cut it
- Put it where the hand is, the section you read while doing the thing
- A line says what to do next time. A line that only says what went wrong is not a fix
- Never add a lessons section, a log, a history or a hard-won list
- Never write the incident. Names, ids, dates and runs die with the session
- The file has a cap. Writing a line means cutting a line. Cut first, the eviction is where the thinking happens
- Then grep the other skills for the rule you changed. One fixed in place and left standing in two is worse than no fix, you now believe it
